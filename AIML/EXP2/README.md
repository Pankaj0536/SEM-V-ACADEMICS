## Steps to Get a Decision Tree : 
step1 : Search for `WEKA` Tool in your system.

step2 : Go to `Explorer` Application (Right side on your Screen)

step3 : Go to `Preprocessor`(Upper side into the Navigation Bar)

step4: Choose your saved file from your system (it should be `.arff` formated)

step5 : Click to `Visualize All`

step6 : Next Go to `Classify` next to `preprocessor` .

Step6 : Go to `test options ` Check `Cross-Validation` Folds : `4 or less than 10 `(Cross-Validation is in Test)

step7 : Click `Start`

step8 : `Right Click` on to your Result. It will show some options - Go to `Visualize Tree`
